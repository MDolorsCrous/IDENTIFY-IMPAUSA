Identify usa el **BFI-2**, y esa skill hoy no lo tiene entre sus instrumentos: lista once
y el Big Five no es ninguno. Esto es lo que hay que añadirle para que Identify entre en el
circuito integrativo como ya lo hace el DISC.

## 3. En `references/tools.md`, sección nueva

```markdown
## 12. BFI-2 (Big Five)

**Origin.** Soto & John (2017), *The Next Big Five Inventory*. Spanish adaptation:
Gallardo-Pujol et al. (2022), *Psychological Test Adaptation and Development*. 60 items,
1–5 scale. Open source; the Spanish form and the scoring key are public (OSF kp572).

**What it measures.** Self-reported behavioural TENDENCIES across five broad domains, each
split into three facets. Stable over time but not fixed. NOT capability, NOT clinical
status, NOT job fit.

**The five domains and their facets.**

| Domain | Facets |
|---|---|
| Extraversión | Sociabilidad · Asertividad · Nivel de energía |
| Cordialidad | Compasión · Respeto · Confianza |
| Responsabilidad | Organización · Productividad · Responsabilidad |
| Emocionalidad negativa | Ansiedad · Depresión · Volatilidad emocional |
| Apertura de mente | Curiosidad intelectual · Sensibilidad estética · Imaginación creativa |

**How to read the data.**

1. **Facets before domains.** Two people with the same domain score can be opposite
   inside it. The reading is in how the three facets split, not in the average.
2. **A facet that separates from the other two is the story** of that domain.
3. **Facets carry less weight than domains.** They are four-item scales; in the Spanish
   adaptation ten of the fifteen fall below .70 reliability. Domain statements can be made
   normally; single-facet statements need hedging.
4. **Bands need norms.** Without a normative sample, "high" means high on the scale, not
   high relative to people. Say which one you are using.

**Interpretation principles.**

- No score is good or bad. A mid score usually means flexibility, not absence.
- «Ansiedad» and «Depresión» are technical facet names, not clinical conditions. A high
  Depresión facet means the person registers low mood more readily — nothing more.
- Combinations carry more than single facets: the published literature describes patterns
  that need several facets at once (see Identify's `combinations.json`, 26 rules with
  citations).

**How this tool connects to other tools.**

- **DISC** — behaviour vs. trait. DISC describes style in context; BFI-2 describes
  tendency. High D with low Cordialidad is a coherent pair; high D with high Compasión is
  worth asking about.
- **Gambrill-Richey** — low Asertividad plus an anxious-inhibited quadrant converge;
  if they contradict, the context is doing the work.
- **PERMA** — Extraversión and low Emocionalidad negativa are the strongest predictors of
  subjective wellbeing. Low PERMA with a favourable profile is a signal about the
  situation, not the person.
- **VIA** — strengths often map onto Apertura and Cordialidad facets.
- **Roda de la Vida** — low Organización plus a low Work area points at systems, not effort.
- **Creences limitants** — a limiting belief usually sits on top of an extreme facet.

**What BFI-2 does not tell us.** Intelligence, values, competence, job fit, clinical
status, or what the person will do. It describes tendency, not behaviour in a given moment.

**When data is incomplete.** The instrument does not score partial responses: all 60 items
are required. If only domain scores are available, work at domain level and say so — the
facet-level reading, which is what BFI-2 adds over a short Big Five, is not available.
```

## Nota

El informe de Identify es de **un solo instrumento** y tiene su propia plantilla. Esta
ficha es para el otro caso: cuando la coach cruza Identify con más herramientas y produce
el informe integrativo de 14 secciones. Los dos conviven, como en Connect.
