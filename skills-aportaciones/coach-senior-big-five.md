# BFI-2 (Big Five)

## Origin

Soto & John (2017), *The Next Big Five Inventory*, the revision of John's 1991 BFI.
Spanish adaptation: Gallardo-Pujol et al. (2022). 60 items, 1–5 scale, ~8 minutes.
Open source: the form and the scoring key are public (OSF kp572).

## What it measures

Self-reported behavioural tendencies across five domains, each with three facets. The
facet layer is what BFI-2 adds over a short Big Five, and it is where the coaching value
sits: two people with the same domain score can be opposite inside it.

## The five domains

| Domain | Facets | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| Extraversión | Sociabilidad · Asertividad · Nivel de energía | Where the person's activation comes from |
| Cordialidad | Compasión · Respeto · Confianza | How they approach others, and how soon they trust |
| Responsabilidad | Organización · Productividad · Responsabilidad | System vs. drive vs. duty |
| Emocionalidad negativa | Ansiedad · Depresión · Volatilidad emocional | Anticipation vs. mood vs. reactivity |
| Apertura de mente | Curiosidad intelectual · Sensibilidad estética · Imaginación creativa | Absorbing vs. generating |

## How to read the data

1. **Read the split, not the average.** A domain at 2.67 made of 1.75 / 3.75 / 2.50 is a
   different person from one made of three 2.67s. Name the facet that separates.
2. **Extremes first.** Facets near the midpoint carry little; work with what is far out.
3. **Combinations over single facets.** The literature describes patterns that need
   several facets at once — power orientation, burnout risk, environmental mastery. A
   single high facet is rarely a finding.
4. **Facets are four-item scales.** In the Spanish adaptation ten of fifteen fall below
   .70. Hedge single-facet statements; domain statements can be made normally.

## Interpretation principles

- No score is good or bad; the same tendency helps or costs depending on context.
- Mid scores usually mean flexibility, not absence.
- Trait is not behaviour. BFI-2 says what tends to happen, not what will happen.

## Common interpretation traps

- **Reading «Depresión» or «Ansiedad» clinically.** They are facet names. High Ansiedad
  is a tendency to anticipate and worry, and when the other two facets sit mid it is
  anticipation, not distress.
- **Reading low Sociabilidad as a deficit.** It is a different source of activation, not
  a social problem, and it is not social anxiety.
- **Prescribing the missing trait.** Telling someone with low Organización to organise
  more rarely works. The lever is usually external structure, not more effort.
- **Using it for selection.** It is not a selection instrument, and this skill's remit is
  coaching.

## Where the frameworks connect

- **Low Asertividad** → the assertiveness framework, especially boundaries, saying no, and
  receiving criticism. Combine with Gambrill-Richey if available.
- **Low Respeto with high Asertividad** → the conflict framework: conflict type, temperature,
  and separating positions from interests.
- **High Volatilidad** → emotional regulation before any difficult-conversation script.
- **Low Confianza** → power dynamics and stakeholder mapping: delegation costs more than
  capability explains.
- **Low Organización with high Productividad** → time and systems, not motivation.

## Useful coaching questions

- When something goes well, is it the drive or was there structure that time?
- Of all the times the anticipation fired this year, how many were right?
- Who do you lower the guard with, and what did they do for that to happen?
- If a system worked on its own, what would the freed energy go to?

## How this connects to other tools

DISC describes style in context, BFI-2 describes tendency — complementary, not redundant.
PERMA and low Emocionalidad negativa converge on wellbeing. VIA strengths usually map onto
Apertura and Cordialidad facets. A limiting belief tends to sit on top of an extreme facet.

## What it does not tell us

Intelligence, values, competence, job fit, clinical status, or what someone will do.

## When data is incomplete

All 60 items are required; the instrument does not score partial responses. With domain
scores only, work at domain level and say so — the facet reading, which is the point of
this instrument, is not available.
