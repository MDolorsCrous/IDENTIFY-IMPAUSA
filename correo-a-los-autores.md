Dear Professors John and Soto,

I am writing to request permission to use the BFI-2 commercially.

My name is Maria Dolors Crous and I run IMPAUSA / LivePausa, a small coaching practice in
Catalonia, Spain. We have built a self-administered tool called **Identify**: a person
answers the BFI-2, the scores are computed, and they receive an interpretive report used
as the starting point for a coaching conversation. It is not used for personnel selection
and it is not presented as a clinical instrument — the report states this explicitly.

We currently use the **Spanish adaptation** (Gallardo-Pujol et al., 2022,
doi:10.1027/2698-1866/a000020), and we would like to add the **original English version**.

I want to be straightforward about the terms. Identify is part of a paid service, so I
understand this falls outside the non-commercial use your site permits, and I would rather
ask than assume. I would be grateful if you could tell me:

1. Whether a commercial licence for the BFI-2 is available, and on what terms.
2. Whether it would cover both the Spanish adaptation and the English original.
3. Whether there is a licence fee, and how you prefer it to be handled.

For context on scale: we expect on the order of [PON AQUÍ UNA CIFRA REALISTA: p. ej.
«a few hundred administrations per year»].

The instrument is already credited in every report we produce — the reference list cites
Soto & John (2017) with its DOI, the Spanish adaptation, and carries the line "BFI-2 ©
Oliver P. John and Christopher J. Soto". If a licence requires particular wording or
placement, I will of course follow it.

Thank you for making the BFI-2 as open as you have, and for your work on it.

Kind regards,

Maria Dolors Crous
IMPAUSA / LivePausa
